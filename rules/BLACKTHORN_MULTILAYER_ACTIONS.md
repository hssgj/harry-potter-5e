# Blackthorn House — vícevrstvé vyhodnocování akcí (herní dodatek)

Status: PLAYER-APPROVED for the Blackthorn House dungeon in The Lost Years, Part IV, 2026-09-20.
Scope: scene adjudication and playtest only. This does NOT activate the draft PR #1 engine, rewrite CORE_v0.2, retroactively modify the Voss interrogation, or create a new universal dice system for other campaigns.

## Principle
One d20 roll does not automatically resolve an entire chapter or every consequence of a complex declaration. Decompose the PLAYER'S actual declared intention into distinct, *mechanically meaningful* facets; each may require its own roll, save, damage die, or no roll. Different outcomes combine into one causal scene. Do not roll reflexively for every sentence or for a facet that is guaranteed, irrelevant, or already resolved.

Before each uncertain roll, GM communicates what is being tested, the die, relevant bonus/defense or DC when public, the stakes and known risk. GM fixes the thresholds and effects before results are known. Player rolls their character's dice; GM resolves NPC rolls, saves and consequences. Do not silently shift difficulties or invent dice results.

## Dice roles for bespoke multilayer improvisation
- d20 — success/control/precision of a discrete uncertain action, contested by an appropriate defense or preselected DC; relevant character modifiers and proficiency per CORE_v0.2. It is NOT an automatic extra control check before every ordinary learned spell.
- d10 — optional *intensity/magnitude* of an improvised magical effect once its scope and allowed maximum were announced. A high result cannot grant abilities or area beyond the character's established repertoire. This is not a universal substitute for spell-card damage dice.
- d8 — physical impact or damage only when the declared action and fiction actually establish a hit, collision, damaging spell or destructive contact, and when the GM predeclared d8 as the applicable effect die. Apply armor/saves/resistances/cover and any environmental effect according to announced mechanics. Do not auto-roll physical damage for mere levitation or speech.
- d6 — optional narrative psychological pressure/stress effect of intimidation or other *specifically declared* mental impact, against appropriate target resolve, circumstances and defenses; it measures effect within stated bounds, NOT automatic psychic HP damage, truth extraction, mind control, confession, or forced NPC behavior. Magical psychic HP damage requires a separate established damaging effect and explicit rule.
Other dice and spell-specific cards remain available when the existing mechanic specifies them. The d10/d8/d6 assignments are Blackthorn-specific adjudication defaults, not four mandatory rolls per action.

## Sustained effects
A successful initial telekinetic manipulation does not mean indefinite free control. Record what is held, concentration/attention, range, location, limits, duration and any cost/strain established before roll. Trigger a fresh check when there is a meaningful change in threat, pressure, scale, concurrent action, resistance, loss of line of sight, injury or emotional control — not merely because another dialogue sentence was spoken. On new checks, state the stakes and consequence of losing control. Respect CORE_v0.2: one meaningful action and movement per turn, at most one reaction; concentration on only one effect; injury-triggered CON concentration save where applicable. A cinematic declaration does not grant several free combat actions.

## Voss interrogation illustration — NOT a retroactive rewrite
Entry: if vanishing Disillusionment at a chosen instant is safe and already established, no roll; if timing under a detection threat is uncertain, separate d20 check. Intimidating spoken approach: social/resolve resolution if the NPC can resist and stakes matter. Raising multiple desks: separate casting/control resolution with declared feasible scale, optional d10 intensity. Holding them through interrogation: track ongoing concentration and recheck only when Voss reaches for his wand, Matthael's fury materially surges, or Matilda interferes; independently resolve Voss's reactions. Physical d8 only upon a real damaging collision. Psychological d6 only for a declared, uncertain fear effect; it does not compel answers. A powerful success in one facet does not automatically succeed at another; a failed facet does not erase prior established success.

## Canon and QA
Record the actual player declaration, rolls, modifiers, defended outcomes, effects, ongoing concentration/cooldowns, injuries and scene consequences in the Blackthorn checkpoint. GM may make a one-off ruling for an unsupported improvisation but must state it before rolling; no engine rewrite mid-expedition. Keep spoiler/GM secrets separate from player-facing notes. On reaching/resolving Archive IX, carry the played consequences back into The Lost Years prose and stop dungeon mode.
