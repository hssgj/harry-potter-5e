Status: ACTIVE PLAYTEST; applies to HOG-WF-01 only. No global override of CORE_v0.2 or Blackthorn-scoped multilayer rules.

## Fair encounter contract
1. Player declares intention/action and position. GM states plausible reach, cost (one meaningful action + movement, one reaction per round), target defense/DC and visible stakes BEFORE rolling. No extra free spell effects from a long cinematic description. Concurrent or sustained effects require available action/concentration and recorded duration.
2. Player rolls player dice; GM rolls/sets NPC attacks, saves, resistance and behavior. Lock DC, phase triggers and damage BEFORE result. Report misses, partial effects, collateral, consumables, HP/Strain, conditions, concentration, reaction and positioning after each exchange. Do not claim opposed rolls, saving throws, HP losses or successes without actual resolution.
3. Use CORE_v0.2 for known learned spells and turn structure. d20 Control + optional declared Effect die + d6 Strain/Backlash is a THIS-ENCOUNTER improv overlay inherited from recent play; no automatic d20+effect+strain for a standard learned spell unless meaningful bespoke facets exist. Player's latest roll 17/1/2 already applied to daisy snare, not a new turn.
4. At each round boss has its own initiative/action and at most its stated reactions. It actively pursues contested crystal, reacts to positioning, guard breaks, magic, terrain, and attempts to flee; cannot be auto-pacified by a single social roll, but an earned nonlethal tactical shutdown is possible.
5. Three phase progression is requested, but numbers and phase thresholds are NOT canonical: use visible telegraphs and record concrete triggers when agreed before combat. Do not treat suggested Matthael lvl22/boss lvl37, boss 72 HP or armor 3 as confirmed stats. Read current sixth-year character sheet if available; mark any absent current HP, modifiers, cooldowns or current strain UNKNOWN rather than reverting to obsolete first-year HP13/level1. For an immediate unresolved action, set local bounded stakes and request relevant dice instead of inventing a full stat sheet.
6. Resist repetitive exploits with fictionally established adaptations, not arbitrary immunity or changing DC after successful rolls. Creative environment uses matter; a successful partial effect creates openings but cannot erase a multi-part encounter. Natural 20 cannot achieve impossible outcomes, natural 1 cannot predetermine entire battle.
7. Maintain player agency: clear attack tells, avoid silent hard counters, telegraph disabling/serious injuries and opportunity to respond. Falling, water pressure, taking an exposed crystal, boss attacks and using the plant trap may impose genuine costs. 0 HP uses rules/CONSEQUENCES.md, not forced instant death or consequence-free escape.
8. Victory condition: boss actually disabled, repelled, bypassed for good via earned multi-step solution, or genuinely disengaged with meaningful pursuit consequences. Merely reaching waterfall or temporarily rooting boss is NOT a boss clear. Reward must follow achieved state; key may be physically carried while encounter remains ACTIVE.
9. Distinguish (A) relic key already seized but contested, (B) distinct earned boss-clear payoff at THIS POI, and (C) distant map reward behind key-linked POI. Neither B nor C is granted automatically. C remains location/contents undisclosed until found in play.
