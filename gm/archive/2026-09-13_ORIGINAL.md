# Slytherin Matthael — campaign checkpoint
Updated: 2026-09-13

Source of authority: user's decisions in the current campaign-planning conversation. This file distinguishes our campaign canon from published Wizarding World lore.

## Approved campaign canon
- Player character: Harry Potter / Slytherin version of Matthael. Preserve his identity; do not import abilities, resources or events from Frostfire or Shinobi campaigns.
- Entry age: sixteen in the approved origin scene. Late magical education after childhood in a secluded island family living in the Muggle world.
- Era: Hogwarts Legacy, with Professor Eleazar Fig and Professor Matilda Weasley. Not Harry Potter's school generation. Exact starting date still open; 1890–91 is the proposed calendar anchor.
- Island: remote island; Tahiti/its surrounding region is the user's suggested inspiration, not a finalized island name or exact location.
- Family has suppressed its magical ancestry for generations from fear of what its blood carries. External inherited magical seal, domestic observances and a worn object maintain suppression. Descendants no longer fully understand the procedures.
- Matthael does not consciously reject his own magic. Do not automatically assign him an Obscurus.
- Bloodline has an unusual relationship with magical boundaries and possible passages through them. This is original campaign lore. It is not automatic mastery of teleportation, unrestricted ward breaking, or an established 5e ability.
- A final attempt to renew the failing family seal causes the origin incident. During the ritual Matthael reaches for his childhood snake, caught on the other side of the closing seal. His escape is unconscious magic; the rupture of the restraining enchantment wounds him.
- Scar: user's exact clarification is “od ucha po krk až na liční kost.” It involves the ear/neck/cheekbone region. Side of body and exact shape remain unspecified. It comes from the ritual/escape above; no new scar power is assigned.
- Escape destination: a road near Hogsmeade; injured Matthael is found by a Hogwarts professor. Which professor and why this destination remain unassigned. Do not treat the long-distance event as ordinary repeatable Apparition.
- Black snake companion has been with him since childhood. It is already a black Occamy, concealed in a small snake form by an old enchantment in our campaign version. Later revelation reveals its identity, rather than transforming an ordinary species into another. Name, origin and who enchanted it remain open. Matthael initially does not know it is an Occamy.
- Retained visual identity: dark hair, pale complexion, black/deep-green Slytherin aesthetic; ebony wand with red motif and dragon essence. This records the established visual concept, not a claim that “dragon essence” is a standard published wand core. Wand statistics, acquisition and precise construction remain open.
- Antagonist premise: someone knows the ancestry/curse and wants to exploit Matthael's power and ambition to cause terrible harm to the wizarding world.
- Approved story direction: influential patron offers genuine useful instruction, protection and recognition while preparing Matthael for a task involving a magical protection the patron cannot overcome alone. Patron identity, public role and exact plan remain open. Do not silently designate Fig or Weasley as the villain.
- Dimension Door crossover connection desired; its details are not established here and must not be invented from another Matthael's story.

## Canon boundaries
Hogwarts Legacy setting and established characters provide the background; Matthael's ancestry, seal, patron plot and concealed black Occamy are our original campaign additions.
Hogwarts Legacy is set in the nineteenth century. Around 1890 Albus Dumbledore is already a child; he starts Hogwarts in 1892. “Before Dumbledore” here means before his Hogwarts attendance/career, not before his birth.
Using the game's era does not yet commit us to replaying its main quest or inheriting its protagonist's Ancient Magic powers.
