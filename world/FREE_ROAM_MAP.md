# DISCOVERY STATE MODEL
Possible marker states:
- KNOWN
- ???
- DISCOVERED
- RESTRICTED
- CLEARED / RESOLVED (only when play justifies it)

Discovery principles:
- `???` marks an approximate investigation area, not a visible secret door.
- Clues may come from perception, Revelio, dialogue, physical interaction, prior information or creative magic.
- A successful roll reveals only what that action could reasonably reveal.
- High rolls do not auto-solve secrets.
- New shortcuts, access states and persistent consequences remain changed after discovery.

# DYNAMIC ENCOUNTER RULE
Default exploratory encounter check: d6 when entering a new meaningful area or after extended exploration.
- 1-2: meaningful encounter
- 3-4: minor event / environmental cue / opportunity
- 5-6: quiet passage

Context always overrides randomness. A school corridor does not spawn an implausible hostile fight merely because of a low roll.

# LOOT PRINCIPLES
- Common finds, useful gear, rare items and one exceptional reward slot may exist per region.
- Exceptional does not automatically mean stronger.
- Unique loot can change exploration options, appearance, interactions or tactics.
- Loot identity is not exposed on the world map before discovery.
- Nothing is added to Matthael's inventory until actually obtained in play.
